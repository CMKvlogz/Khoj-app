Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Khoj ("we", "our", "the platform") is a community-run missing persons registry for Pakistan. This page explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used.

1. Information we collect

When you submit a missing person report or a sighting report, we collect the information you choose to provide, which may include:

  • Name, age, gender, and description of the missing person
  • Photos of the missing person
  • City, last-seen location, and home address
  • A contact phone number for the reporting family
  • A security PIN you set, used only to verify you later when marking a case as Reunited
  • Your device's precise location (GPS), if you allow location access, to help verify the authenticity of a report

2. Why we collect it

This information is used solely to operate the missing persons registry: to publish reports so the community can help locate missing individuals, to route sighting reports back to the reporting family, and to verify the authenticity of reports and reduce false or fraudulent submissions.

3. Location data

If you grant location access, we record your device's GPS coordinates at the time a report or sighting is filed. This location is used only for internal verification purposes and is visible only to the Khoj admin team — it is never shown publicly. You can decline location access; this will not prevent you from filing a report.

4. Who can see your information

Missing person reports (name, photo, description, last-seen location) are visible to the public, as the purpose of the platform is to help the community assist in the search. A family's home address and direct contact number are only shown to users who have submitted a sighting for that specific case, in order to protect the family's privacy.

5. Data retention

Reports remain on the platform to preserve the historical record of missing and reunited cases, unless removed by our team for a valid reason (such as a confirmed false report).

6. Your rights

If you would like a report you filed corrected or removed, please contact us using the details below.

7. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, please reach out through the contact information provided on the platform.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
